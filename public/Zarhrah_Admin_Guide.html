Introduction & Security Architecture

The Zarhrah Executive Panel is the private control suite for the boutique, accessible at /admin. Every modification made within the panel is immediately dispatched in real time to customer storefronts worldwide via Firestore cloud synchronization.

Access Protocols

  • Navigate to /admin on the boutique domain.
  • Authenticate using authorized staff credentials (Email & Password via Firebase Auth).
  • Accounts not enrolled in the staff roster are immediately blocked with an Access Denied gate, and the unauthorized attempt is recorded in the immutable audit log.

Role-Based Access Hierarchy

  • Super Admin (Owner): Unrestricted global permissions across all tabs, Staff management, Database Soft Resets, and Executive Analytics.
  • Manager: Full management of Catalog, Orders, Customers, Pricing AI, Layout, and Customer Recovery. Restricted from deleting staff or resetting the database.
  • Editor / Associate: Catalog inventory updates, order status fulfillment, and restock notification monitoring.
⚠ 15-Minute Session Inactivity Guard
To protect customer transaction records and financial telemetry, the panel enforces an automated 15-minute inactivity timeout. A visual security modal warns you 60 seconds prior to expiration, allowing you to click "Stay Logged In" or logout safely.

Theme & Home Layout CMS (Deep Dive)

What "Theme & Layout" Means — The Visual Storefront CMS

In standard e-commerce setups, rearranging a storefront landing page requires developer intervention, template code edits, or heavy third-party page builders that degrade site performance. In Zahrah, the Theme & Layout tab is an enterprise-grade, in-house visual Content Management System (CMS) that puts complete creative control into the hands of boutique directors, brand stylists, and merchandisers.

Every toggle, image upload, and product curation in this tab is backed by Cloud Firestore real-time persistence (layoutConfig). Changes sync instantly with zero downtime across all active client browsers—no code deployments, rebuilds, or server restarts are required.

⚜ Strategic Merchandising Purpose
The primary objective of the Theme & Layout tab is visual storytelling and conversion pacing. Luxury customers do not simply browse raw database inventories; they respond to curated aesthetics, seasonal drops, editorial model pairings, and narrative hierarchies. This tab allows you to sculpt that journey turn-by-turn.

Global Storefront Mode: The Catalog Visibility Switch

At the top of the Theme & Layout dashboard sits the master Home Page Configuration control. Clicking this switch toggles between two distinct retail presentations:

A
Catalog Visible (Standard Retail Mode — Default)

When enabled (Catalog Visible with a gold indicator), the full product catalog displays at the bottom of the landing page, complete with category tabs, search filtering, and pagination. Ideal for day-to-day shopping when customers expect broad inventory exploration.

B
Catalog Hidden (Exclusive Editorial / Lookbook Mode)

When disabled (Catalog Hidden), the generic product grid is completely suppressed from the home page. The storefront transforms into a minimalist, magazine-style editorial experience consisting exclusively of your curated carousels, muse banners, and styling lookbooks. Ideal for exclusive fashion drops, private atelier events, or campaign launches.

How to Use the Intelligent Searchable Product Selector

Previously, selecting products for a carousel required scrolling through an unorganized list of hundreds of SKUs. The upgraded Intelligent Searchable Product Selector makes curation seamless, fast, and visual:

1
Real-Time Live Search

Type any search term into the search bar. The selector instantly filters the catalog across Product Title, Designer Brand, and Custom Tags. Type "silk", "black", or a designer name to narrow results immediately. Clear your search instantly with the ✕ button.

2
Instant Category Filter Pills

Click any category pill (ALL, Clothing, Shoes, Bags) to restrict the view to that specific department without losing your active selections.

3
The "Selected (X)" Audit Filter

Click the Selected (X) pill in the top-right corner. The grid instantly collapses to display only the pieces currently linked to that section. This provides an immediate, foolproof visual audit of what will appear on the storefront. Click it again to return to the full catalog.

4
One-Click Checkmark Selection & Auto-Save

Click any product card to toggle it on or off. Selected items immediately illuminate with a high-contrast gold checkmark badge (✔) in the top-right corner, a refined dark border ring, and an info overlay showing item title and formatted Naira price (₦XX,XXX). Every click writes straight to Firestore in the background—no "Save All" button required.

How to Create & Curate "Styling Ideas" (Lookbooks)

The Styling Ideas module is one of Zahrah's highest-converting features. Rather than selling standalone garments, it allows merchants to present holistic luxury outfits that shoppers can purchase in a single click:

1
Expand the Styling Ideas Section

Locate the full-width Styling Ideas card in the Curated Collections area and click "Manage Looks" to expand the editor.

2
Spawn a New Look

Click the gold "Add New Look" button at the bottom. A new ensemble card appears labeled Look 0X.

3
Upload the Editorial Photograph

Hover over the image preview box and click the upload icon to upload a high-resolution, full-body styled model photo (3:4 portrait ratio recommended). The image uploads directly to Cloudinary and links to the look.

4
Configure Title & Total Ensemble Price

Set an evocative, high-fashion title in the Look Title field (e.g., "Mayfair Evening Velvet Ensemble") and enter the combined package price in the Total Price field (e.g., ₦245,000).

5
Link Matching Catalog Artifacts

Under "Products in Look", use the embedded compact product selector to search and click the exact items comprising the outfit (e.g. velvet blazer + tailored trousers + leather loafers). Linked pieces appear in the Matched Products preview bar.

6
Sequence & Reorder

• Reorder Look Priority: Use the vertical ↑ and ↓ buttons on the left of each look card to determine which outfit appears first on the storefront.
• Reorder Garment Sequence: Hover over any item in the Matched Products bar and click the ← or → arrows to adjust how pieces are presented in the customer checkout popover.

Deploying On-Demand Dynamic Custom Carousels

Need a special carousel for a flash promotion or fashion week? You do not need developer assistance:

1
Enter Section Title

Scroll down to the "Deploy Section" card. Type your desired carousel heading into the input bar (e.g., "SUMMER LINEN EDIT" or "PRIVATE VAULT SELECTIONS").

2
Deploy Instantly

Click the black "Deploy Section" button. The section immediately appears in the layout hierarchy and in the admin audit log.

3
Curate & Control

Use the section's embedded Searchable Product Selector to pick the items to showcase. Toggle visibility with the eye icon, or permanently remove the section anytime with the trash icon.

💎 Visual Merchandising Rules of Thumb
  • Optimal Carousel Density: Curate between 6 and 12 pieces per carousel. Fewer than 4 pieces makes the carousel look sparse on large desktop screens; more than 16 pieces causes customer scroll fatigue.
  • Portrait Aspect Ratio (3:4): Ensure all catalog and lookbook images follow a consistent 3:4 portrait aspect ratio (e.g. 1200 × 1600 px) with neutral or studio backgrounds for uniform visual luxury.
  • Graceful Fallback Logic: If a merchant leaves a section visible but selects 0 products, the storefront automatically falls back to displaying the newest catalog arrivals for that category, ensuring the site never shows a blank void.
  • Pre-Campaign Audit: Before launching promotional newsletters or influencer links, open each active section in the Theme & Layout tab, click Selected (X), and verify that all featured items are currently in stock with positive inventory counts.

Card-by-Card Storefront Controls Reference

The Theme & Layout console is structured into distinct functional tiers. Below is the operational procedure for every card on the interface:

1
Master Control: Home Page Configuration (Catalog Visible / Hidden)

Located at the very top of the page. Clicking this card toggles between Standard Catalog Mode (full bottom product catalog with filters) and Editorial Drop Mode (only curated lookbooks, muse showcases, and themed carousels are displayed). The indicator dot turns gold when active.

2
Hero Carousel Card (Top-of-Funnel Flagship Slides)

• Show/Hide: Click the eye icon to enable or disable the entire hero banner.
• Edit Slides: Click the "Edit Slides" button to expand the slide drawer.
• Upload Imagery: Click "Add Slide" or upload directly to Cloudinary. Recommended dimensions: 1920×900px (desktop wide) or 1200×1600px (mobile portrait editorial).
• Delete Slide: Hover over any slide in the manager and click the red trash icon.

3
Features Bar (Trust & Service Pillars)

Displays the three pillars of Zahrah luxury: 100% Authentic Luxury, White-Glove Delivery, and Paystack Escrow Guarantee. Click the eye button to toggle visibility. Keep active during general retail operations to reinforce buyer trust.

4
Boutique Banner Card (Mid-Page Editorial Statement)

• Headline: Enter an uppercase editorial title into the text field (e.g. "PARIS ATELIER '26" or "SILK & VELVET ARCHIVE").
• Banner Image: Paste a direct image URL or click the upload icon to upload from your local drive to Cloudinary.
• Storefront Display: Renders as an immersive full-bleed horizontal divider with subtle parallax typography.

5
Lifestyle Showcase Card (Dual-Muse Split Photography)

• Edit Muses: Click "Edit Muses" to open the dual-photo management drawer.
• Left Muse (Accessories): Upload a portrait editorial photo highlighting footwear, bags, fragrances, or jewelry.
• Right Muse (Streetwear / Couture): Upload an editorial photo highlighting tailored fits, blazers, shirts, or tracksuits.
• Storefront Role: Displays side-by-side on desktop and stacked on mobile, driving category navigation with high aesthetic impact.

6
Manor Collection vs. Bundles & Deals (Curated Product Rows)

• Manor Collection (Royal Tier): Reserved for your highest-ticket luxury artifacts. Click "Select Products" to open the Searchable Product Selector, choose 6–10 premium pieces, and verify them under the "Selected" pill.
• Bundles & Deals: Highlight promotional pairings, perfume gift boxes, or multi-item sets. Both sections support independent visibility toggles.

7
Curated Categories (Tabbed Interactive Collection)

• Tab Management: Click "Edit Tabs" to rename tabs (e.g. rename "Slides" to "Footwear"), adjust display order, or hide underperforming categories.
• Assign Pieces: Click any category tab (T-Shirts, Pants, Jackets, Shorts, Slides, Bags) and pick exactly which products appear when shoppers click that tab on the homepage.

Three Strategic Merchandising Workflows

How to configure the Theme & Layout tab for different business events:

⚜ Scenario 1: Launching a New Seasonal Collection Drop

  • Step 1: Expand Hero Carousel → Upload 2–3 new high-resolution seasonal campaign slides.
  • Step 2: Update Boutique Banner → Set title to "AUTUMN/WINTER '26 COUTURE" with new backdrop.
  • Step 3: Open Deploy Section → Enter "THE NEW ARRIVALS EDIT" and click Deploy.
  • Step 4: Use the Searchable Product Selector to pick the 8 newest pieces for the new section.
  • Step 5: Keep Catalog Visible enabled so shoppers can browse full inventory after viewing the new drop.

⚜ Scenario 2: Switching to VIP Private Atelier Lookbook Mode

  • Step 1: Click Home Page Configuration → Toggle to Catalog Hidden.
  • Step 2: Open Styling Ideas → Curate 4–6 complete head-to-toe luxury outfits with editorial imagery and package prices.
  • Step 3: Open Manor Collection → Curate exclusively high-margin royal couture pieces.
  • Step 4: Result: The home page acts like a digital fashion magazine with no clutter, driving maximum perceived brand prestige.

⚜ Scenario 3: 48-Hour Weekend Flash Sale / Bundle Promotion

  • Step 1: Open Bundles & Deals → Click the eye icon to make it Visible.
  • Step 2: Click "Select Products" → Search and check all discounted pairings and gift sets.
  • Step 3: In Boutique Banner → Set headline to "48-HOUR ATELIER PRIVILEGE EVENT".
  • Step 4: When the event ends, toggle Bundles & Deals off with a single click—all product selections remain saved for the next sale.

Client Retention & Abandoned Checkouts

The Abandoned Carts tab captures incomplete checkouts in real time to empower sales recovery.

Automated Recovery Architecture

  • Client Session Tracking: When a shopper enters their contact details on the checkout page, a recovery session is debounced after 3 seconds of inactivity.
  • Paystack Drop-Off Detection: If a client closes the payment modal without paying, the session is logged under Abandoned Carts.
  • Automated Luxury HTML Email: The system automatically sends a branded luxury recovery email showcasing the exact items, sizes, and prices left behind, with a direct 1-click button to resume checkout.
  • Manual Recovery: Admins can click "Send Recovery Email" or copy the customer's WhatsApp number for personalized concierge outreach.

Restock Requests & Demand Forecasting

Under the Restock Requests tab, merchants monitor shopper demand for sold-out pieces.

  • When an item is sold out, visitors can click "Notify Me When Available" and provide their email or WhatsApp.
  • The requests are grouped by product, providing an immediate restocking priority list based on customer volume.
  • Once inventory is replenished in the Products tab, clicking "Notify Waitlist" dispatches automated HTML restock emails with product photography to all subscribed shoppers.

Database Governance, Sitemap & Recovery

The sidebar features emergency database governance tools to protect store data integrity.

Sidebar Database Governance Suite

  • Soft Reset / Wipe Database: Clears transactional test data (orders, view logs, abandoned sessions) while preserving catalog products, user accounts, and settings. Automatically creates a restoration backup.
  • Restore Database: Reverts the last soft reset, restoring orders and abandoned carts instantly from the backup collection.
  • Automated XML Sitemap (/sitemap.xml): Auto-generated during each build to index 280+ active products and pages in Google and Bing with Google Image Search schemas.
  • Robots Policy (/robots.txt): Directs web crawlers to public collections while blocking administrative routes (/admin, /checkout, /orders).
⚠ CAUTION ON DATABASE OPERATIONS: Database resets should only be performed by Super Admins before collection launches or after stress-testing. All database operations are permanently logged in the Activity Log.
