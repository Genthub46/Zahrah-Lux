03

Step-by-Step Mobile Configuration

🍎 Option A: Apple iPhone & iPad (Apple Mail App)
  1. Open Settings on your iPhone and scroll down to tap Mail.
  2. Tap Accounts → Add Account → Select Other → Tap Add Mail Account.
  3. Enter your basic details:
    • Name: Zahrah Luxury Admin
    • Email: admin@zarhrahluxurycollections.com
    • Password: Your assigned mailbox password
    • Description: Zahrah Corporate Mail
  4. Tap Next. Make sure IMAP is highlighted at the top tab.
  5. Under INCOMING MAIL SERVER:
    • Host Name: mail.zarhrahluxurycollections.com
    • User Name: Enter your full email address (e.g. admin@zarhrahluxurycollections.com)
    • Password: Your assigned password
  6. Under OUTGOING MAIL SERVER (Do not leave username or password blank even if marked optional):
    • Host Name: mail.zarhrahluxurycollections.com
    • User Name: Enter your full email address
    • Password: Your assigned password
  7. Tap Next (the device will verify the SSL certificate), ensure Mail is toggled ON, and tap Save.
🤖 Option B: Android Phone (Gmail App or Samsung Email)
  1. Open the Gmail or Samsung Email app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile avatar (top right corner) → tap Add another account.
  3. Choose Other from the email provider list.
  4. Type your full corporate email address → tap Manual setup → select Personal (IMAP).
  5. Enter your account password and tap Next.
  6. In Incoming server settings:
    • Server: mail.zarhrahluxurycollections.com
    • Port: 993  |  Security: SSL/TLS
  7. In Outgoing server settings:
    • Require signin: YES (Checked)
    • SMTP Server: mail.zarhrahluxurycollections.com
    • Port: 465  |  Security: SSL/TLS
  8. Tap Next → select your sync frequency → finish by naming the account.
🌐 Option C: Instant Webmail Access (Any Mobile Browser)
  1. Open Safari or Google Chrome on your phone.
  2. Navigate directly to: https://rogue.hkdns.host/roundcube
  3. Enter your full email address and password to immediately view inboxes, compose emails, and manage contacts with zero configuration.
Security & Best Practices: Always ensure SSL/TLS is enabled for both Incoming and Outgoing servers. If sending fails on standard port 587 due to mobile cellular network restrictions, switch Outgoing Port to 465 with SSL/TLS. Never share mailbox credentials through unencrypted channels.
