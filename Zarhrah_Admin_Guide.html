Introduction & Staff Login

Welcome to the Zarhrah Executive Panel (located at /admin). This is the control room of your online luxury boutique. Everything you do here — whether uploading a new dress, changing a price, or updating a banner — appears immediately on the live website for your customers in Nigeria and across the globe.

How to Log In

  • Open your web browser and go to /admin on the boutique website.
  • Type in your registered staff email and password.
  • Only staff members enrolled in the system can enter. If an outsider tries to log in, the system blocks them immediately with an Access Denied notice.

Staff Roles & Permissions (Who Can Do What)

  • Super Admin (Owner / Executive Director): Has full access to everything in the store, including adding new staff, managing payments, and system maintenance.
  • Manager (Store Manager): Can add/edit products, manage customer orders, view daily sales, update homepage banners, and follow up on abandoned carts. Cannot delete other staff members.
  • Editor (Sales / Warehouse Associate): Can update stock numbers, mark customer orders as shipped, and check restock alerts.
⚠ 15-Minute Safety Auto-Logout
To keep your store's money records safe, the system logs out automatically if the computer is left untouched for 15 minutes. A warning box pops up on your screen 60 seconds before this happens. If you are still working, simply click "Stay Logged In" to continue.

The Main Dashboard (Quick Overview)

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in. It gives you an instant, bird's-eye view of how the boutique is performing today.

What Each Number Means

  • Total Revenue (₦): The total amount of money paid by customers through Paystack since the store opened, displayed clearly in Nigerian Naira (₦).
  • Revenue This Month: Total sales made in the current calendar month.
  • Active Products: The total number of items currently live and visible for customers to buy on the website.
  • Registered VIP Customers: The total number of shoppers who have created personal customer accounts on your boutique.
  • Total Orders: The cumulative count of all customer orders placed so far.

Stock & Order Warning Badges

At the top of the dashboard, you will see bright visual badges alerting you to things that need immediate attention:

Out of Stock Alert — Items that have completely finished (Stock = 0). Click to see what needs restocking.
Low Stock Warning — Items running out fast so you can order more before they finish.
Pending Orders — New customer orders waiting for your team to package and send out.

Live Store Analytics (Visitors & Performance)

The Analytics tab connects directly to Google Analytics 4 (G-N2CS95WEY2). It lets you see who is browsing your store right now and which luxury items are generating the most interest.

Quick Google Analytics Buttons

  • 🟢 Live Indicator: A green light confirming that customer activity on the website is being recorded properly in real time.
  • ⚡ Real-Time ↗: Opens Google Analytics to see exactly how many people are browsing your website this very second.
  • 📊 Traffic ↗: Shows you where your visitors are coming from — such as Instagram ads, WhatsApp links, Google searches, or direct visits.
  • 🛍️ Sales ↗: Shows your checkout conversion rate and how much money your marketing campaigns are generating.
  • Date Range Filter: Easily switch between 7 Days, 14 Days, and 30 Days to see your sales growth.

Understanding Your Profit & Sales

  • Total Revenue: Total gross sales received in Naira (₦).
  • Gross Profit: The actual profit your store made after deducting the purchase cost of the clothes from the sales price.
  • Profit Margin (%): Shows how profitable your boutique is as a percentage.
  • Average Order Value (AOV): The average amount of money a customer spends each time they check out (e.g. ₦85,000 per order).
  • Most-Viewed Pieces Leaderboard: A live top-5 ranking showing the exact clothes customers are clicking and looking at most often, with pictures and stock counts. Use this to know what to display on the front page or restock first!

Managing Products & Different Prices for Sizes

All clothes, shoes, bags, and perfumes are managed inside the Products tab. You can add new arrivals, edit prices, update stock, and upload photos.

How to Set Different Prices for Different Sizes (e.g. Size L vs XXL)

In luxury fashion, some sizes (like XXL, bespoke tailored sizes, or items with extra fabric) cost more to produce. The system lets you set special prices for specific sizes with ease:

1
Turn on Sizing

When creating or editing a product, switch on the "Sizing" toggle and choose your sizes (e.g., S, M, L, XL, XXL).

2
Enter the Custom Size Price

Next to each size, you will see a box labeled "Size Price (₦)". If you leave it empty, the size will use the normal product price (e.g., ₦40,000). If you type 45000 for XXL, that size will sell for ₦45,000.

3
Instant Live Update on the Website

When a shopper views the product and taps "XXL", the price on their screen changes instantly and smoothly. When they check out, Paystack charges them the exact price you set.

Stock Velocity Badges (How Fast Items Are Selling)

The system tracks how fast pieces are being bought over 30 days and gives you simple color tags:

> 2 Weeks Supply — Plenty of stock remaining.
~X Days Left — Selling fast; order more from your supplier soon.
< 7 Days Critical — Almost sold out; urgent attention needed.
Sold Out — Item finished (Stock = 0). Customers see a "Notify Me" restock button.

Customer Orders & Paystack Payments

The Orders tab lets you track all purchases from the moment the customer pays through Paystack until the package reaches their doorstep.

How Paystack Payments Work

Customers can pay using their Nigerian debit cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve), Direct Bank Transfer, or USSD code. Once the payment is successful on Paystack, the order enters the system automatically with the transaction reference, customer address, phone number, and purchased items.

The 3 Order Delivery Stages

1. Pending / Processing → 2. Shipped / Dispatched → 3. Delivered

  • 1. Pending / Processing: The customer has paid. Your store or warehouse team should pick the clothes, inspect them for luxury quality, and pack them neatly in Zarhrah luxury packaging.
  • 2. Shipped / Dispatched: The package has been handed over to your courier or dispatch rider (for same-day Lagos delivery or interstate delivery to Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, etc.).
  • 3. Delivered: The rider or courier confirms the parcel is in the customer's hands. Changing the order status to "Delivered" automatically sends the customer a final confirmation email.

Theme & Home Layout: Designing What Shoppers See

What is "Theme & Layout"?

In many standard websites, changing a homepage banner or moving clothes around requires hiring a web developer. In Zarhrah, the Theme & Layout tab puts complete design control directly in your hands. You can turn sections on or off, change banners, upload photos, and pick which clothes appear on the homepage — all with a single click, without writing a single line of code!

Best of all, every change you make saves automatically and immediately. Customers browsing your website see the new look right away with zero downtime.

🧪 Safe Sandbox Playground: Test Layouts Without Affecting Live Shoppers

Worried about making a mistake or want to play around with new arrangements before showing real customers? At the top of the Theme & Layout tab, you will see the Sandbox Playground Control Bar.

How Safe Sandbox Playground Protects Your Live Store:

  • Enter Safe Sandbox: Click the gold button "Enter Safe Sandbox Playground". The control bar turns amber and activates isolated mode.
  • Risk-Free Designing: Reorder sections, turn banners on or off, edit lookbooks, and select featured gowns. None of these changes touch your live store! Real shoppers on zahrah-boutique.web.app keep seeing the unchanged live website.
  • Refresh From Live: Click "Refresh From Live" anytime to reset your sandbox with an exact, fresh copy of what is currently live.
  • Publish to Live Store: When you are 100% happy with your new design and tested how it looks on mobile and desktop, click "Publish to Live Store". The system will ask for confirmation and immediately deploy your new layout to live shoppers!
  • Exit Sandbox: Click "Exit Sandbox" anytime to return to standard mode.

The Master Switch: Catalog Visible vs. Catalog Hidden

At the very top of the Theme & Layout page is the Home Page Configuration card. Clicking it switches between two store modes:

A
Catalog Visible (Normal Everyday Shopping Mode — Default)

When active (Catalog Visible with a gold light), the full catalog with all 23 categories (Shirts, Shoes, Perfumes, Pants, etc.) displays at the bottom of the homepage. Ideal for regular day-to-day shopping when customers want to explore your entire collection.

B
Catalog Hidden (Exclusive VIP Lookbook Mode)

When turned off (Catalog Hidden), the long product grid at the bottom is hidden. Your homepage transforms into an exclusive, minimalist fashion magazine showing only your curated lookbook outfits, muse photos, and luxury campaign banners. Perfect for private drops, fashion week launches, or VIP events.

How to Pick Clothes Fast with the New Compact Selector

We recently upgraded the product selector to make it fast, small, and simple. You no longer have to scroll through huge cards:

1
Instant Live Search

Type any keyword in the search box (e.g. "silk", "jacket", or "black"). Results filter immediately as you type. Click the ✕ button to clear your search.

2
Department Dropdown

Instead of 23 long buttons taking up space, simply tap the clean Category Dropdown to pick any section (e.g. Shirts (29), Perfumes (40), Shoes (33)).

3
The "Selected (X)" Button

Want to see only the clothes you have already chosen for this section? Click the Selected (X) pill. The list instantly filters to show only your picked items so you can double-check everything.

4
1-Tap Add / Remove & Instant Auto-Save

Every product is shown as a neat, compact row with its photo, name, category, price, and stock count. Click "+ Add" to select it; it turns into a black button with a gold checkmark ("✔ Selected"). Everything saves straight to the website — no "Save All" button needed!

3 Practical Scenarios: How to Set Up Your Store for Different Events

Here are step-by-step guides for everyday situations in your boutique:

⚖ Scenario 1: You Just Received New Stock (Launching a New Collection)

  • Step 1: Go to Hero Carousel → Click "Edit Slides" and upload 2 or 3 beautiful campaign photos of the new clothes.
  • Step 2: Go to Boutique Banner → Type the title (e.g. "AUTUMN/WINTER '26 ARRIVALS") and upload a matching photo.
  • Step 3: Go to Deploy Section → Type "NEW IN ATELIER" and click the black Deploy button.
  • Step 4: Click the new section's product selector and pick the 8 newest pieces.
  • Step 5: Make sure Catalog Visible is turned on so customers can also browse the rest of your store.

⚖ Scenario 2: Switching to VIP Fashion Lookbook Mode (Exclusive Event)

  • Step 1: At the very top, click Home Page Configuration → Set to Catalog Hidden.
  • Step 2: Go to Styling Ideas → Click "Manage Looks" and ensure 4 to 6 complete luxury outfits are active with model photos and combo prices.
  • Step 3: Go to Manor Collection → Pick your most premium, high-margin pieces.
  • Step 4: Result: Your homepage looks like a luxury Vogue magazine — zero clutter, maximum prestige!

⚖ Scenario 3: Running a 48-Hour Weekend Flash Sale or Special Deal

  • Step 1: Go to Bundles & Deals → Click the eye icon so it turns black (Visible).
  • Step 2: Click "Select Products" → Pick all the discounted pairings, perfume sets, or promotional clothes.
  • Step 3: Go to Boutique Banner → Type "48-HOUR FLASH PRIVILEGE EVENT".
  • Step 4: When the weekend ends, simply click the eye icon on Bundles & Deals to turn it off. All your selections remain saved for the next sale!
💎 Visual Merchandising Rules of Thumb for Staff
  • Best Number of Items: Select between 6 and 12 pieces per carousel. Fewer than 4 looks empty on a computer screen; more than 16 makes customers tired of scrolling.
  • Use Portrait Photos (3:4): Always upload portrait photos (taller than they are wide, e.g. 1200 × 1600 px) with clean, well-lit backgrounds so your store looks uniform and expensive.
  • Check Stock Before Promoting: Before sending out Instagram broadcasts or WhatsApp newsletter links, open each active section, click Selected (X), and make sure none of the featured clothes are sold out!

Abandoned Carts: Following Up with Shoppers

An Abandoned Cart happens when a customer adds clothes to their bag, starts filling out their name and delivery address at checkout, but closes Paystack without paying.

How to Recover Lost Sales (Automated + WhatsApp Follow-Up)

  • Automatic Luxury Email: The system automatically sends the customer a beautiful branded email showing the exact clothes they left behind, with a button to complete payment.
  • WhatsApp Direct Outreach (Highest Success Rate in Nigeria): Under the Abandoned Carts tab, you will see the customer's name, email, and phone number. Your customer care team can copy the phone number and send a polite WhatsApp message:
    "Hello [Name]! We noticed you were picking some items from Zahrah Luxury today. Did you experience any network difficulty with your payment? We would be happy to assist or reserve your pieces for you!"
    This single strategy recovers substantial revenue each month!
8

Restock Requests (When Items Sell Out)

When a popular shoe, shirt, or perfume runs out of stock (Stock = 0), customers browsing the website will see a button that says "Notify Me When Available".

  • Shoppers click the button and leave their email or phone number.
  • In the Restock Requests tab, all requests are grouped by product, showing you which sold-out pieces have the highest customer demand.
  • When new supply arrives, go to the Products tab, update the stock number (e.g. from 0 to 15), and click "Notify Waitlist". The system automatically sends an email to all interested buyers letting them know the piece is back!

Managing Staff Accounts & Activity History

Super Admins can manage team access under the Staff tab to ensure full accountability across the business.

  • Inviting Team Members: Add authorized staff email addresses and assign their role (Super Admin, Manager, or Editor).
  • Removing Staff: If an employee leaves the company, remove their email immediately so they can no longer log in.
  • Activity Logs (Who Did What?): The Activity Logs tab records every single important action — who added a product, who changed a price, who marked an order as shipped, and who edited homepage banners, complete with their email and the exact time.

System Safety, Backup & Google Search (SEO)

The sidebar contains safety tools designed to keep store data protected and ensure your boutique appears on Google search.

Sidebar Safety & Search Tools

  • Soft Reset / Wipe Database: Clears out test transactions (orders, cart sessions, view counts) while keeping all your 259 products, photos, user accounts, and settings 100% safe. Always creates a safety backup first.
  • Restore Database: Reverses the last reset immediately, bringing back orders and cart history from the backup.
  • Automated Google Search Sitemap (/sitemap.xml): Updates automatically every time the website builds, indexing all 280+ clothes and pages so shoppers in Nigeria and overseas find you when searching for luxury fashion on Google.
⚠ IMPORTANT WARNING FOR STAFF: Never click "Wipe Database" unless specifically instructed by the business owner. All database operations are recorded permanently in the Activity Log.
